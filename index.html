Export Information

Domestic Shipping

We provide reliable domestic shipping services across India, ensuring your flower orders reach destinations in perfect condition. Our domestic network covers major cities and towns with timely delivery schedules.

International Export

Our international export services handle the complete logistics chain from India to overseas destinations. We specialize in perishable cargo handling and compliance with import regulations.

Our Export Process

  1. 1 Enquiry Submit your flower requirements and specifications
  2. 2 Order Confirmation We confirm availability, pricing, and delivery timeline
  3. 3 Packaging Expert packaging using international standards for perishables
  4. 4 Dispatch shipment tracking information
  5. 5 Delivery Delivery to your location with condition confirmation
